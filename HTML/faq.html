Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
All information provided in this FAQ section is for educational and demonstration.

1. What is EcoMart?

EcoMart is a coursework based eco-friendly e-commerce website designed to showcase sustainable products through simple web pages. It demonstrates our understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, UI/UX design, and wireframing tools like Balsamiq. The project focuses on promoting green living while applying practical web development skills.

2. Is EcoMart a real shopping webiste?

No, EcoMart is not a real shopping website.
EcoMart is a project or coursework to simulate an eco-friendly online store. It showcases sustainable products, demonstrates your web development and design skills, and highlights eco-conscious values, but it does not have real online shopping features like payment processing, real user accounts, or live product inventory.

3. What type of products are available on EcoMart?

EcoMart features a variety of eco-friendly and sustainable products that promote green living. These include items like:

  • Reusable water bottles (bamboo, stainless steel)
  • Bamboo combs and grooming products
  • Eco-friendly towels made from natural fibres
  • Reusable tote bags
  • Bamboo furniture
  • Other biodegradable, recyclable, or sustainable household items

4. Can I placed a real order on EcoMart?

No,you cannot place a real order on EcoMart.
EcoMart is a coursework or project website created to demonstrate web development and eco-friendly product concepts. It does not have real shopping features such as payment processing, live inventory, or order fulfillment. Users can view products and explore pages, but cannot complete a real purchase.

5. Why does EcoMart promote eco-friendly products?

EcoMart promotes eco-friendly products to encourage sustainable living and reduce environmental impact. By offering items made from natural, renewable, and biodegradable materials, the platform helps raise awareness about conscious consumption, reduces waste, and supports a healthier planet. The project highlights the importance of making eco-conscious choices in daily life while combining web development skills with environmental responsibility.

6. What technologies are used to build EcoMart?

EcoMart is built using front-end web development technologies, including:

  • HTMLfor structuring web pages and content
  • CSSfor styling the website and making it visually appealing
  • JavaScriptfor adding interactivity and dynamic features
  • VS Codeas the code editor to write and manage the project
  • Balsamiqfor creating wireframes and planning the website layout

7. Is EcoMart a real online store?

No, EcoMart is a prototype created for coursework purposes. It demonstrates product display, website design, and eco-friendly concepts but does not support real orders or payments.

8. How does EcoMart help the environment?

By promoting products made from renewable, biodegradable, or recyclable materials, EcoMart encourages users to reduce waste, conserve resources, and adopt sustainable lifestyles.

9. Can I contact EcoMart for support?

Since EcoMart is a coursework project, there is no real customer support. However, the project may include a contact page to show how such features would work in a real website.
